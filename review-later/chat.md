Classic causes of portal venous gas include ischemia (e.g., SMA or SMV thrombosis, low-flow ischemia) and mechanical factors (e.g., volvulus, obstruction).

Portal venous gas is peripheral, as opposed to pneumobilia, which is centrally located in the liver. Mesenteric ischemia is biggest concern: intraluminal gas perforates the bowel wall, due to bowel wall breakdown, CT findings include pneumatosis, submucosal edema, ascites.

Predisposing factors: Primary Ischemia – SMA or SMV thrombosis, low flow ischemia and Mechanical - volvulus and obstruction with secondary bowel ischemia. There are also several non-ischemic etiologies of portal venous gas.
