02 / DURING THE BLOCK

Match tonight to tomorrow.

Get these local details on day one
  • Confirm the schedule, expected resident role, and which BI-RADS v2025 terms are implemented in reporting templates.
  • Ask for the service's screening/risk pathway, diagnostic protocols, BI-RADS 3 follow-up process, and comparison-image workflow.
  • Before procedures: review the local consent, antithrombotic, anesthetic, device, specimen, clip, aftercare, and concordance policies with your supervisor.
  • Find out whether the department or institutional library already provides the complete BI-RADS manual. The free summaries do not include its full examples or FAQs.

The daily loop · 20–30 minutes

Pick one question from the day → read one targeted section → interpret 3–5 cases before revealing answers → explain the management decision → revisit the miss in 48 hours. Use your existing daily learning / Notion study route for a small number of durable learning points.

03 / CURRENT LANGUAGE

BI-RADS v2025, without buying a book first.

Official ACR sources

Start with the free ACR update and modality summaries. Keep the relevant summary beside older books and quizzes. This is an orientation and study index; it does not reproduce the full manual.

Changes to recognize in older teaching

Areav2025 orientation
MammographyLobulated shape returns; microlobulated margin becomes indistinct. Use layering rather than milk of calcium. Coarse includes former popcorn-like/dystrophic terms. Describe interval change separately from asymmetry.
UltrasoundAdds non-mass lesions and lobulated shape; mixed solid and cystic replaces complex cystic and solid. The mammography margin change must not be applied automatically to US.
MRIFocus is removed as a finding category; T2 mass signal is added. Indistinct replaces irregular as a margin term. NME multiple regions is removed.
Reporting / CEMCategory 0 distinguishes additional imaging from prior comparisons. Reports are harmonized; CEM is an integrated section.

Orientation paraphrased from ACR What's New and the linked modality summaries. A terminology change alone does not establish benignity or alter the need to evaluate a finding.

Assessment → a specific plan

Practice the distinction: 0 incomplete; 1 negative; 2 benign; 3 probably benign (≤2%); 4 suspicious (>2% to <95%; 4A/4B/4C); 5 highly suggestive (≥95%); 6 known biopsy-proven malignancy. Category 3 means planned surveillance after appropriate evaluation; categories 4 and 5 generally lead to tissue diagnosis. Check the modality summary and supervising service for the exact recommendation.

A 30-minute update pass

  1. 10 min: read the ACR change document.
  2. 10 min: compare the mammography and US summary forms with five older quiz descriptors.
  3. 10 min: describe one mammo/US case and one MRI case in current language.

Older-language flags in existing quizzes

Search results can include explanations and non-lexicon uses of words such as “focus.” Check the case and modality before interpreting a flag.

Source limitations worth knowing
  • The Core Review diagnostic PDF export is only 100 pages and stops before the answer section. Its existing 151-entry app uses the complete EPUB.
  • The two regulatory variants both have 66 entries. The newer PDF variant reuses the EPUB text with PDF figures; they are alternate presentations of the same bank.
  • Original quiz image alignment has build notes, but this inventory did not re-audit every image or answer. Use the source when something looks mismatched.
  • Older regulations, screening recommendations, high-risk lesion management, and terminology need current-source checking. A Medality “Content Reviewed” date does not establish that every lesson was rewritten for v2025.
