“Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.” — Psalm 119:105

Teacher flow
  1. Let both children try with the small light. Allow a fall or two; every fall automatically returns them to the beginning.
  2. Ask: “What would make this easier? What helps us know where to go in real life?” Read Psalm 119:105.
  3. Have each child press the Light button (1 / Numpad 1), or use “Turn on the Lord's light for both.” The visible area expands dramatically, but the maze itself does not change.
  4. After they reach the goal, emphasize: the Lord does not always remove the path, choices, or challenges. His light helps us see the next steps and where the path leads.
