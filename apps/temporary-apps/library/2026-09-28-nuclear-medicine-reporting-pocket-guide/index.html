Pocket guide

Y-90 / Radioembolization Planning MAA

Core checklist

1. Intrahepatic distribution

Where the MAA localizes and whether the distribution is expected for the selected arterial territory.

2. Extrahepatic activity

Look specifically for nontarget deposition outside the liver.

3. Lung shunt

Report the calculated percentage lung shunt / lung shunt fraction.

4. SPECT/CT + CT findings

Use the SPECT/CT for localization; a brief low-dose noncontrast CT findings section is common.

Reusable wording
The calculated percentage lung shunt is 3.65%.
The lung shunt is calculated to be 3.65%. No extrahepatic activity.
Expected distribution of radiotracer activity within the liver without evidence of extrahepatic radiotracer activity.
No significant radiotracer activity is seen outside of the liver.
Focal extrahepatic radiotracer activity is present along the gastric wall, concerning for nontarget arterial perfusion.
