# Checkpoint review

Author visual review, 2026-09-22. This is a first teaching prototype, not independent clinical expert validation. All 70 targets were inspected against the actual case contact sheets, including interval endpoints. Small vessel targets marked "region" test the check area rather than delineating a vessel lumen. The scan includes only the superior maxillary sinuses and ends at the craniocervical junction.

Targets are case-specific landmark patches, not whole-organ segmentations. Clicks elsewhere in a correctly identified structure may not score. Practice Locate shows the scored slice and region. Scalp at the vertex narrows rapidly, so its two points use a single reviewed slice. All added edge tolerance is zero mm.

Window rationale: brain W50–130/L20–60 emphasizes gray-white and CSF; soft W60–450/L15–100 permits brain or wider soft-tissue views; wide W150–450/L30–120 provides extra-axial review; bone W1500–4000/L250–1000 uses the separate bone reconstruction. These are teaching ranges, not prescribed diagnostic settings.

Corrections during review: scalp vertex and lateral positions; high-convexity sulci; gray-white junctions; ventricular bodies, septum and occipital horns; transverse and superior sagittal sinus regions; retrobulbar optic nerves; frontal sinus; superior maxillary sinuses.
