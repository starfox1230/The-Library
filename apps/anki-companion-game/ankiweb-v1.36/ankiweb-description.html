Speed Streak adds a timer and streak to desktop Anki reviews. It is meant to help you answer faster and keep moving when a card is taking too long.

New: Time Boost

Time Boost uses charges instead of points.

  • Answer cards to earn charges.
  • Before the timer runs out, press R to spend one charge and add time.
  • You can change the number of charges, the number of cards required to earn one, and the amount of time each charge adds.

If you find yourself pausing or undoing reviews to keep your streak, you can also turn on No Pause, No Undo, or both. Charges then become a limited way to give yourself more time.

Turn it on or change it: Settings → Gameplay

Hover over the charges to show the No Pause toggle, No Undo toggle, and Boost shortcut. Click the charges or progress bar to open the related settings. Click the shortcut key to change it.

Legacy Points

The previous point and multiplier system is now called Legacy Points. It is still available. You can switch between Legacy Points and Time Boost at any time in Settings → Gameplay.

New visuals

Singularity and Crystal Reactor are new visual options. Click the visual button at the bottom-left of Speed Streak to switch between Sphere, Singularity, Crystal Reactor, and Brick.

Other features

  • Separate question and answer timers
  • Optional timer rules for specific note types, tags, typed-answer cards, and AnKing one-by-one cards
  • Time Drain and Review Later flags
  • Review Later Manager
  • Optional audio and controller haptics
  • Inline side panel or separate external window
  • Saved external-window positions

Notes:

  • Desktop Anki only
  • Legacy Points remains available after updating
  • No Pause and No Undo are optional
  • Your existing settings are preserved
