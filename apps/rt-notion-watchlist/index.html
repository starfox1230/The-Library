Save now. Pick a movie later.

Add to Watchlist saves the title and Rotten Tomatoes link to your existing database, marks it For Later, and records the date and Movie/TV Show type. Then open the entry in Notion to fill in the rest.

Download the Chrome extension ↓

Personal unpacked extension. Not published in the Chrome Web Store.

Three steps to your next movie.

  1. Unzip the download. Keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent.
  2. Load it in Chrome. Type chrome://extensions into the address bar, enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
  3. Connect Notion once. The setup screen walks you through creating a Notion connection, sharing your Watchlist with it, and entering its secret locally. After connecting, refresh Rotten Tomatoes.

Open your Watchlist ↗

Make it yours in Notion.

New entries appear in For Later · Quick Add, newest first. Your interest ratings, reviews, streaming platform, and thoughts are ready for you to fill in. The extension checks for saved links and flags possible older matches.

The Notion secret stays in this Chrome profile’s local extension storage. It is never in this download or sent to Rotten Tomatoes. No analytics. No hosted server.
