Connect once

  1. Open Notion connections and create an internal connection called “Next Up” in the workspace containing your Watchlist.
  2. Give it Read content and Insert content capabilities. No user information is needed.
  3. In Notion, open the Watchlist as a full page. Use ••• → Connections → Add connections → Next Up to grant it access. You can also grant access from the connection’s content access settings.
  4. Copy the connection secret into the field below. It stays in this Chrome profile. Don’t paste it into chat.

Saved locally in Chrome extension storage, which is not an encrypted vault. It is never synced to your Google account, included in downloads, or given to Rotten Tomatoes.

After connecting, refresh an open Rotten Tomatoes movie or TV page. Look for the Add to Watchlist button at the bottom right. Saved entries have an Open in Notion link for your ratings, interest, platform, and thoughts.
